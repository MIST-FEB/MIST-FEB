Powering Progress with MIND ID

Powering Progress with MIND ID

Hello, OptiMIST People!

Back at it again with KaMIST, your weekly dose of insights into the latest marketing trends.

This week, we’re stepping into the strategic side of industry with Mining Industry Indonesia, or MIND ID.

As Indonesia’s state owned mining holding, MIND ID integrates national mineral assets into one coordinated ecosystem. What makes it interesting from a marketing perspective is how it positions itself not just as a mining operator, but as a driver of national transformation.

MIND ID consistently frames its role around integration, hilirisasi, and long term value creation. Instead of focusing only on production numbers, it highlights downstream development, industrial strengthening, sustainability, and governance. Through the spirit of “MIND ID for Indonesia and The World,” the brand connects natural resources to a bigger mission: national prosperity and global competitiveness.

This positioning elevates the conversation. By emphasizing sustainability, collaboration, and future industries, MIND ID shifts from being seen as a resource manager to a creator of long term value. That shift strengthens stakeholder trust, from government to investors to the public.

In conclusion, MIND ID proves that even in heavy industry, branding matters. When a company consistently communicates its broader purpose, it builds long term relevance.
